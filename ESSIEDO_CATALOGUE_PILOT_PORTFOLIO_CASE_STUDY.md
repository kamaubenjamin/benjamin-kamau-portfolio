# Essiedo Catalogue Pilot — Portfolio Case Study

## Positioning

**A client pilot exploring whether a structured web catalogue can improve the handoff between Instagram/WhatsApp product discovery and product-specific WhatsApp enquiries without replacing the seller's existing social-selling workflow.**

**Status:** Live Pilot / Validation Stage

**Live Pilot:** <https://essiedo-catalogue-pilot.pages.dev>

## Existing Sales Workflow

Essiedo already sells through WhatsApp and Instagram. Social posting remains useful for product discovery and marketing, and customer conversations continue through the seller’s established channels.

The pilot does not attempt to replace that workflow with a conventional ecommerce website.

## Pilot Product Decision

The catalogue adds a structured browsing layer between social discovery and a WhatsApp enquiry. Customers can filter available pieces, identify an exact product and continue into WhatsApp with the selected item carried into the enquiry.

The pilot is validating whether this handoff adds enough value to justify a deeper seller-management V2.

## Current Pilot Scope

- Structured catalogue browsing
- Filtering available pieces
- Identifying a specific product
- Product-specific WhatsApp enquiry handoff
- Responsive live catalogue experience

## Observed Pilot Activity

Observed activity is kept separate from measured business outcomes:

- Essiedo has opened and reviewed the live catalogue.
- The seller has started sharing the catalogue link through existing social channels.
- Seller questions and feedback surfaced possible V2 requirements.
- A separate user suggestion surfaced possible size-guidance functionality.

This activity does not establish sales increases, conversion improvement, customer adoption volume, revenue or successful commercial validation.

## Potential V2 — Not Implemented

- Seller-managed uploads
- Marking one-off items sold
- New-arrival publishing
- Offer pricing
- Size guidance or a size chart

These are possible next-stage requirements only. None are presented as current pilot capabilities.

## Current Status and Boundaries

Essiedo Catalogue Pilot is live and in validation. The seller has reviewed it and begun sharing the link, while feedback is being collected to determine whether deeper seller-management tooling is justified.

No sales, conversion, revenue, workload-reduction, productivity or customer-volume outcome is claimed. The catalogue complements rather than replaces the existing WhatsApp and Instagram workflow.
